Residential Construction

New builds, additions, whole-home renovations, basements

New Construction

Building from the ground up is the chance to get every detail right from the beginning.

PSI manages new residential construction from early planning and site preparation through framing, systems, finishes, and final completion — working alongside architects, engineers, designers, subcontractors, and owners to keep every moving piece organized and accountable.

Build it correctly. Communicate clearly. Put our name on it.

Home Renovations & Large-Scale Remodels

A successful renovation makes an existing home work better without losing what made it worth renovating.

We handle comprehensive renovations involving multiple rooms, structural changes, new layouts, upgraded mechanical systems, and complete interior transformations — coordinated as one project with a single point of responsibility. When framing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, millwork, and finishes all happen in the same project, small communication failures become expensive problems. We maintain visibility across every phase so decisions made in one stage work with what comes next.

The result is a home that feels cohesive — not a collection of separate renovations completed at different times.

Home Additions

Whether you’re adding bedrooms, expanding a kitchen, building another floor, or substantially increasing the footprint, we plan the entire property — structure, exterior architecture, interior flow, mechanical systems, and natural light — so the transition between old and new feels intentional.

Basement Build-Outs & Finishing

A finished basement is one of the most cost-effective ways to add real square footage. We address the fundamentals first — moisture control, insulation, ceiling height, egress, mechanical relocation, code compliance — then finish the space to the same standard as any other floor of the home.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Kitchen Renovations

It shouldn’t just look better. It should work better.

Bathroom Construction & Remodeling

Outdated bathroom — or no bathroom at all — we build the space around your needs.

Complete renovations plus creation and relocation of bathrooms: plumbing relocation, framing, electrical, waterproofing, tile, fixtures, ventilation, cabinetry, and finishes. From luxury primary baths to compact powder rooms and commercial facilities, the fundamentals stay the same — efficient layouts, durable construction, proper waterproofing, clean finishes.

No bathroom? No problem. We can build one.

Structure: Concrete & Framing

Footings, foundations, slabs, engineered truss packages

Concrete & Foundation Work

Everything above the foundation depends on the foundation.

Excavation coordination, footings, foundation walls, slabs, basement floors, garage pads, and exterior flatwork. Elevations, drainage, waterproofing, reinforcement, and backfill are handled as one scope — because problems at this stage are the most expensive ones to correct later.

Framing & Structural Carpentry

Framing sets the accuracy of every trade that follows.

Framing for new construction, additions, remodels, and structural alterations — load-bearing modifications, beams, headers, and openings. We coordinate engineered floor and roof truss packages from layout through setting, so the structural package arrives correct and installs on schedule.

Get the structure right and everything after it goes easier.

Commercial & Multifamily

Commercial framing, buildouts, multifamily construction

Commercial Construction & Framing

Commercial work depends on speed, organization, and coordination at scale.

On a large commercial project our work is part of a much larger sequence — a contractor who falls behind or fails to communicate affects everyone who follows. Our teams are managed accordingly: accurate execution, dependable scheduling, clear communication with the other trades on site.

PSI pairs experienced construction management with custom project technology — progress, outstanding items, and documentation tracked so our team always knows what’s happening and what happens next. It’s integrated into how we operate and comes at no additional cost to our clients.

Better information. Better coordination. Better construction.

Multifamily Construction & Renovation

Rental properties wear differently than private homes. We build for that.

Materials, fixtures, layouts, and construction methods selected for real-world tenant use — units that are attractive to tenants, efficient to maintain, resistant to unnecessary damage, and laid out to maximize usable square footage and privacy. For owners and developers, that means thinking beyond construction cost alone.

We build for the life of the property.

Institutional & Public Bid Work

PennBid, county vendor lists, architect bid lists

Public and institutional work is held to a different standard — and it should be.

PSI participates in publicly bid construction through PennBid, county and municipal vendor lists, and architect-managed bid lists. These projects carry requirements beyond price: complete and responsive bid submissions, prevailing wage and documentation compliance, insurance and bonding standards, defined schedules, inspection sequences, and formal reporting throughout.

We treat those requirements as part of the job. Our project management and documentation practices produce the paper trail these projects demand while the work stays on schedule — a contractor who is straightforward to award, straightforward to administer, and accountable through closeout.

Qualified. Responsive. Accountable through closeout.

How We Build

A walkthrough, from bare ground to keys.

Real photos from PSI job sites — the sequence every project follows, and what we’re paying attention to at each stage that you’d otherwise never see.

01 / 06

Chapter 01 — Site & Foundation

Every build starts below grade.

Excavation, footings, and foundation walls are laid out to the survey and verified before a single wall goes up. Elevations, drainage, waterproofing, and backfill are handled as one scope — because mistakes at this stage are the most expensive ones to fix later.

Chapter 02 — Structure

Framing sets the accuracy of everything after it.

Straight walls, square corners, correct rough openings. We coordinate engineered floor and roof truss packages from layout through setting, so the structure arrives correct and installs on schedule — and no trade downstream inherits a problem.

Chapter 03 — Envelope

Before finishes, the building has to be dry.

Sheathing, wrap, and flashing are sequenced so the structure goes weather-tight as early as possible — protecting the framing, insulation, and every dollar of finish work that follows.

Chapter 04 — Systems

What’s behind the walls matters as much as what you see.

Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC are roughed in to plan and inspected before they disappear. Organized runs, labeled panels, mechanicals laid out for future service — not just for installation day.

Chapter 05 — Finishes

Finishes are where budgets are won or lost.

We source cabinetry, tile, and fixtures around the client’s design and budget — achieving the intended look without unnecessarily increasing project cost. Tile goes on waterproofed, flat substrates. Cabinets are set level and scribed to the room.

Chapter 06 — The Finished Product

Delivered clean, documented, and ready.

The same lot from Chapter 01. We take pride in what we build and show it at every opportunity — our finished projects are the strongest representation of PSI.
