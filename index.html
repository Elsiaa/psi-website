How We Build

A walkthrough, from bare ground to keys.

Real photos from PSI job sites. Six chapters.

01 / 06

Chapter 01 · Site & Foundation

Every build starts below grade.

Layout verified to the survey before a single wall goes up. Mistakes here are the most expensive ones to fix later.

Chapter 02 · Structure

Framing sets the accuracy of everything after it.

Straight walls, square corners, correct openings. Engineered truss packages arrive right and install on schedule.

Chapter 03 · Envelope

Before finishes, the building has to be dry.

Weather-tight as early as possible. A dry building protects every dollar of finish work.

Chapter 04 · Systems

What’s behind the walls matters as much as what you see.

Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC inspected before they disappear. Labeled panels, organized runs, built for future service.

Chapter 05 · Finishes

Finishes are where budgets are won or lost.

Cabinetry, tile, and fixtures sourced to your design and budget. Set level, scribed to the room.

Chapter 06 · The Finished Product

Delivered clean, documented, and ready.

The same lot from Chapter 01. Our finished projects are the strongest representation of PSI.
